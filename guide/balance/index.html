Balance over batches (Fig. S1)

balance = design.balance() · vf.plots.balance(balance)

What it answers

Is the label tied to the batch? If so, recognising the batch is enough to predict the label.

How it works

  • Repertoires per batch and label level
  • Bias-corrected Cramér's V between batch and label: balanced (< 0.1), imbalanced (0.1–0.5), confounded (≥ 0.5)
  • Each batch is mixed-label (more than one label level) or single-label

Reading the result

  • Only mixed-label batches allow a within-batch evaluation
  • Zaslavsky (TRB, disease): confounded (Cramér's V 0.93); 4 of 11 batches are mixed-label: the HIV study, the T1D biobank and the two adult lupus batches

In the manuscript (Fig. S1)

  • Repertoires of each disease class per batch, in the Mal-ID data
  • Identifiers for library preparation or sequencing runs weren't available, so each study was treated as one batch
  • Only HIV, T1D and lupus had mixed-label batches, so only those could be evaluated within batch; COVID-19, influenza and healthy controls came from single-label batches
  • The T1D mixed-label batch held only three healthy repertoires
  • Recommendation: process samples of the different labels in the same batches
